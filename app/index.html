    Roadmap to Platinum

    Four stages, in order. Story first — it unlocks the map, the contacts and the systems everything else needs. Then activities, then collectibles and the long grinds, then cleanup and 100% completion.

    1. Stage 1 — Story (trophies 2–13, 22–24, 26–27, plus 44, 48, 49)

      Play the story in order: opening mission Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1–4 (Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby!, Top Of The World, Ma), the named missions Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory, and the final mission (You Gotta Know When to Get Off, gold). Along the way finish the stranger strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone — toward the 8 strands of Strangers & Freaks.

      Do the missables now: In The Beginning… (cocktail on Ocean Beach, early), Actions Speak Louder (choices build toward a count of 500 — choose whenever the game offers a choice, and keep a save before big decisions), and Bad Luck, I Guess (succeed on a second try after a failure — retry a failed mission instead of reloading an old save).

    2. Stage 2 — Activities

      Try every activity once (Master of None, 9 types), then work the counted ones: 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 14 Top Fun drone race wins, 20 different race wins, fishing vs Cal, one arcade game, bowling at Kolt & Associates, the Bret hunt, 5 takedowns in one Combat Focus, the VCIA departure, the jewelry store, the armored truck, the showroom smash and the melee drive-by. Partner trophies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy — 3 shops back-to-back) need your partner with you.

    3. Stage 3 — Collectibles & grinds

      Explore all 52 areas (The Goodtime State) and fold the travel into the other counts: 100 WAiNK locations, 18 meditation spots, 3 Collectible Card sets, 100 vehicles discovered, 10 Scooter Bros registrations, 8 garage properties, 50 challenges, 5 bank vaults, one illegal operation for each of the 9 gangs, $250,000 laundered in Marked Bills and $100,000 spent in shops.

    4. Stage 4 — Cleanup & 100%

      Finish the remaining stranger strands to reach 8, check the in-game completion percentage for A Long Road (gold, 100% completion), then earn the last stragglers. When 54 trophies are earned, Vice Got a Price! — the platinum — pops on its own.

    Missable trophies — 3

    • In The Beginning… — cocktail with a view on Ocean Beach. Do it early, as soon as free roam opens.
    • Actions Speak Louder — choices made, counting toward 500. Make choices throughout the story; keep a save before major decisions.
    • Bad Luck, I Guess — second time's the charm. Fail, then succeed on the retry.
